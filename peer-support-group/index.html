A space to share and support each other

Every six weeks we offer free, online peer support gatherings. We meet for 90 minutes on a Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday evening to suit the group.

We welcome occasional or regular attendance and you can bring anything you wish, be it a client case or a practical, broader discussion point. There is no need to be currently working as a therapist or space holder.

Meetings are confidential and all material is of course anonymous. It is a relaxed and friendly place for us to build a community of practice and support each other.
