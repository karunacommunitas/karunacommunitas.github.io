Integration Circles

Making space for what continues to unfold.

Integration is the ongoing process of making sense of psychedelic experiences and bringing what we learn into everyday life. Our Integration Circles offer space to explore that process in community, through reflection, conversation and creative practice.

Each gathering has its own focus, inviting connection with ourselves, one another and what continues to unfold.
