Integration Circles

Making space for what continues to unfold.

Integration is the ongoing process of making sense of psychedelic experiences and bringing what we learn into everyday life. Our Integration Circles offer space to explore that process in community, through reflection, conversation and creative practice.

Each gathering has its own focus, inviting connection with ourselves, one another and what continues to unfold.

Psychedelic Integration Through NeuroDynamic Breathwork®

Wednesday 28 October 2026 · 18:30–20:30 GMT
Online via Google Meet

Explore NeuroDynamic Breathwork®, a conscious connected breathing practice accompanied by specially curated music, as a way to support psychedelic integration or inner exploration. The session includes an introduction and Q&A, a breathwork practice, and a sharing and integration circle.

Seeing the System: An Embodied Exploration of Psychedelic Experiences with Coaching Constellations

Wednesday 25 November 2026 · 18:00–19:30 GMT
Online via Google Meet

Explore relationships, roles and patterns through a gentle introduction to coaching constellations. After grounding and an introduction to systemic thinking, use small objects to explore a manageable question about a transition, relationship, work or purpose. The session includes reflection in small breakout groups and time to re-ground.

Facilitated by Sophie, a coaching psychologist and Systemic Coaching Constellations Practitioner whose work draws on somatic, relational and systemic perspectives.

No previous experience is needed. Bring a notebook and a handful of small household or natural objects, with tabletop or floor space within reach of your screen. You choose what to share, and observing, pausing or passing is welcome.

Coaching constellations are not therapy or a substitute for individual support. Choose a topic you feel resourced to explore and plan something supportive afterwards.

Coming Home to the Body — A Somatic Workshop for Psychedelic Integration

Monday 21 December 2026 · 18:00–19:30 GMT
Online via Google Meet

Explore how insights from psychedelic and other non-ordinary experiences can find a place in everyday life through this 90-minute, trauma-informed workshop. Begin with gentle grounding and awareness of bodily sensations, then explore your experience through compassionate, parts-informed inquiry.

The session includes breathwork, gentle micro-movement and somatic exploration, closing with simple practices to carry into daily life. All levels of experience are welcome.

Facilitated by Kavita Golia, a trauma-informed embodiment coach, breathwork facilitator and psychedelic guide, and the voice behind the Tune.In podcast. Her work brings together somatic awareness, breathwork and compassionate inquiry.
